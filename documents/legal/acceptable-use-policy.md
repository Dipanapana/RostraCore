# RostraCore Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Effective Date:** 1 March 2026
**Version:** 1.0
**Provider:** Blaq Cooperation (Pty) Ltd ("RostraCore", "we", "us", "our")

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of the RostraCore Platform and forms part of the Terms and Conditions. By using the Platform, you agree to comply with this AUP.

---

## 1. Purpose

This AUP ensures that the RostraCore Platform is used responsibly, lawfully, and in a manner that maintains security, performance, and reliability for all users.

---

## 2. Permitted Use

You may use the Platform for:

- Managing security guard and employee records within your organisation
- Generating and managing shift rosters for your security operations
- Tracking time and attendance for your workforce
- Calculating payroll and generating payslips for your employees
- Managing client contracts, sites, and billing
- Generating invoices and financial reports
- Storing operational documents related to your security business
- Using the mobile application for guard check-ins and incident reporting
- Exporting data for your legitimate business purposes
- Integrating with the Platform API in accordance with the API documentation

---

## 3. Prohibited Activities

### 3.1 Security and Integrity

You shall not:
- Attempt to gain unauthorised access to the Platform, other user accounts, or related systems
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Platform without prior written authorisation
- Interfere with or disrupt the Platform's infrastructure, servers, or networks
- Introduce malware, viruses, Trojan horses, worms, or other harmful code
- Attempt to bypass authentication, access control, or encryption mechanisms
- Use the Platform to conduct denial-of-service attacks or similar disruptive activities
- Exploit any vulnerability in the Platform for any purpose other than responsible disclosure to us

### 3.2 Data and Content

You shall not:
- Upload or store content that is unlawful, defamatory, obscene, or infringes third-party rights
- Use the Platform to process Personal Information without a lawful basis under POPIA
- Enter false, misleading, or fraudulent data into the Platform
- Use the Platform to facilitate identity theft, fraud, or financial crimes
- Store payment card data (PAN, CVV) in the Platform (use PayFast integration instead)
- Process data unrelated to security workforce management

### 3.3 Reverse Engineering and Competitive Use

You shall not:
- Reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the source code of the Platform
- Copy, reproduce, or create derivative works based on the Platform's design, features, or functionality
- Use the Platform to develop, train, or improve a competing product or service
- Benchmark the Platform's performance for publication or competitive purposes without prior written consent
- Scrape, crawl, or use automated tools to extract data from the Platform beyond what the API provides

### 3.4 Account and Access

You shall not:
- Share Account credentials with unauthorised persons
- Create multiple Accounts for the same organisation to circumvent usage limits or pricing
- Impersonate another user or organisation
- Allow third parties to access the Platform through your Account without our prior written consent
- Use another user's Account without proper authorisation
- Transfer your Account to another entity without our written consent

### 3.5 Resource Usage

You shall not:
- Make excessive API calls that degrade Platform performance for other users (rate limits apply)
- Store excessively large files that are unrelated to workforce management
- Use the Platform for cryptocurrency mining, batch processing, or computation unrelated to its intended purpose
- Automate interactions with the Platform in a manner that places unreasonable load on our infrastructure

### 3.6 Legal and Regulatory

You shall not:
- Use the Platform for any purpose that violates South African law or the laws of any applicable jurisdiction
- Use the Platform to facilitate violations of the BCEA, LRA, or other employment legislation
- Process data in a manner that violates POPIA or other data protection legislation
- Use the Platform in connection with any sanctioned entity or prohibited activity
- Fail to comply with applicable PSIRA regulations in your use of guard management features

---

## 4. Account Security Responsibilities

### 4.1 Password Requirements

- Use strong, unique passwords for all Accounts
- Do not reuse passwords from other services
- Change passwords immediately if a compromise is suspected
- Enable any additional security features offered by the Platform

### 4.2 User Management

- Grant the minimum level of access required for each Authorised User's role
- Remove access promptly for employees who leave your organisation or change roles
- Regularly review Authorised User access and permissions
- Maintain accurate user records

### 4.3 Incident Reporting

- Report any suspected security incident or policy violation to support@rostracore.co.za immediately
- Cooperate with any investigation into a suspected violation
- Preserve any evidence related to a suspected security incident

---

## 5. API Usage

### 5.1 Rate Limits

- API access is subject to rate limiting to ensure fair usage
- Current limits are documented in the API documentation
- Requests exceeding rate limits will receive HTTP 429 responses

### 5.2 API Keys

- API keys are confidential and must not be shared or published
- Rotate API keys immediately if a compromise is suspected
- Do not embed API keys in client-side code or public repositories

### 5.3 Data Access

- Only access data that your Account is authorised to view
- Do not attempt to access data belonging to other organisations
- Respect multi-tenancy boundaries

---

## 6. Monitoring and Enforcement

### 6.1 Monitoring

We reserve the right to monitor use of the Platform to:
- Ensure compliance with this AUP
- Protect the security and integrity of the Platform
- Comply with legal requirements
- Investigate reported violations

### 6.2 Enforcement Actions

Violations of this AUP may result in:

1. **Warning:** Written notice of the violation with a request to cease the prohibited activity
2. **Temporary Suspension:** Suspension of Account access for a specified period
3. **Feature Restriction:** Disabling specific features or reducing access levels
4. **Account Termination:** Permanent termination of the Account and access to the Platform
5. **Legal Action:** Referral to law enforcement or initiation of legal proceedings

### 6.3 Process

- Where practicable, we will provide notice before taking enforcement action
- In cases of serious or ongoing violations, we may take immediate action without prior notice
- We will consider the severity, frequency, and impact of the violation in determining the appropriate response
- You may appeal enforcement actions by contacting info@rostracore.co.za within 14 days

---

## 7. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of any violation of this AUP, please report it to:

- **Email:** support@rostracore.co.za
- **Subject:** "AUP Violation Report"

Reports may be made anonymously. We will investigate all reports in good faith and take appropriate action.

---

## 8. Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability in the Platform, we encourage responsible disclosure:

1. Email security@rostracore.co.za with details of the vulnerability
2. Provide sufficient information to reproduce the issue
3. Allow us reasonable time to investigate and remediate (minimum 90 days)
4. Do not exploit the vulnerability or access data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue
5. Do not disclose the vulnerability publicly before we have addressed it

We appreciate security research conducted in good faith and will not pursue legal action against researchers who comply with this responsible disclosure policy.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this AUP from time to time. Changes will be communicated through:
- Email notification
- In-app notification
- Update to the "Effective Date" above

Continued use of the Platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised AUP.
