## Decisions Made

1. **"Without routing" pattern** - Locale from cookie/localStorage, not URL. Avoids breaking existing URL structure.
2. **Dual storage (cookie + localStorage)** - Cookie for SSR web builds, localStorage fallback for Tauri desktop where cookies may not work.
3. **router.refresh() instead of full reload** - Faster language switching, preserves form state.
4. **Natural translations** - Avoided robotic machine translations. Used proper Afrikaans and Zulu workforce terminology.
5. **PAYE/UIF/SDL stay as acronyms** - Standard terms recognized across all SA languages.
6. **Namespace structure by feature** - Matches Next.js App Router page structure for easy lookup.
